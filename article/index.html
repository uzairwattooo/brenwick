New to Brenwick?

Brenwick reconstructs how a company operates, makes uncertain assumptions explicit, and evaluates the business across thousands of plausible futures. We identify which assumptions change a defined decision outcome and test selected interventions inside the model. The result is conditional on the model and interpreted by a human analyst.
